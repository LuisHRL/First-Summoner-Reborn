# Contributing to First Summoner Reborn

Thank you for your interest in contributing!

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repository.

2. Create a branch for your changes.

3. Make and test your modifications in Unity.

4. Submit a pull request explaining your changes.

## Guidelines

* Respect other contributors.

* Do not submit third-party content without appropriate rights.

* Document important changes.

* Preserve applicable copyright and license notices.

* Respect the project's original history and contributors.

## Project Governance

The original project initiator maintains the official repository and reviews proposed changes.

Forks and independent development are permitted under the applicable open-source licenses.

## Licensing

By submitting contributions, you agree to license your contributions under the applicable project license, unless otherwise agreed.
